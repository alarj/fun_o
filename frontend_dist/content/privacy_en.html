Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how Fun-O processes personal data and other data generated when using the participant user interface.

Fun-O is a web-based environment that can also be used through a mobile application for recreational orienteering competitions and other similar activities.

What this policy covers

This policy covers data processing in the participant user interface, including the participant view used in a web browser and in the Fun-O mobile application.

What data is processed

  • Alias (participant identifier): the participant identifier entered when joining a competition. Participants are not required to reveal their real identity; a freely chosen identifier that is unique within the competition is sufficient.
  • Email address: if the user provides it voluntarily.
  • Competition participation data: which competition was joined, when it was joined, and the history of the user's participation status.
  • Answers and results: answers submitted at checkpoints, points earned, timestamps, visited checkpoints, and result summaries.
  • Location data: if required by the competition logic or map view, the device location is processed to enable competition functionality.
  • Technical device and session data: the application uses cookies and local storage to maintain session state, language selection, progress, and map view state.

Why data is used

  • to allow joining a competition;
  • to distinguish one participant from other participants in the same competition;
  • to display questions, receive answers, and calculate scores;
  • to display the map view, checkpoints, and the participant's location where this is allowed or required by the competition;
  • to display results, rankings, and participation history;
  • to ensure technical operation, security, and troubleshooting of the application.

Use of location data

Fun-O may use the device location only for competition functionality. This may include, for example, showing the participant's location on the map, centering the map on the participant, checking whether the participant is within the permitted area for answering a question, or storing the participant's location at the time of answering in order to support later result analysis and route calculation.

Not all competitions use location data in the same way. In some competitions, the application may still work without location permission, but some features may then be limited or unavailable. The use of location data depends on the specific competition and is decided by the competition organizer when creating the competition.

Cookies and local storage

The application uses technical cookies and browser or app local storage. These are used, for example, to keep the user session, language selection, progress, and map view state.

Collected data, including technical data, is not used in the participant user interface for third-party marketing purposes.

Who data is shared with

  • Competition organizer: the organizer may see participant data, answers, and results related to their own competition.
  • Technical operation of the application: data is processed in the services and systems used to keep Fun-O operational.
  • Other third parties: data is not sold to third parties and is not shared with third parties for marketing purposes. Data may be processed only through services that are necessary for the technical operation and administration of Fun-O.

Data retention

Competition-related data may be retained for results, history, statistics, and the reliability of the system. The exact retention period may depend on the nature of the specific competition, choices made by the organizer, and the needs of system administration.

User rights

Users have the right to request correction or deletion of data concerning them to a reasonable extent, taking into account the needs of competition management, result display, and system operation.

If you want your data to be corrected or deleted, first contact the relevant competition organizer or the Fun-O administrator at fun-o@gmail.com.

Data security

Fun-O uses reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect data against unauthorized access, modification, loss, or destruction. However, no internet-based service is completely risk-free.

The application uses servers located in the European Union, and data processed and stored through Fun-O is kept only on servers located in the European Union.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the functionality of the application, the scope of data processing, or the legal framework changes. The updated version will be published on the same page.
